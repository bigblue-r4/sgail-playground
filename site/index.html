Hidden Text X-Ray

Text can look harmless to a person and carry something else to an AI model: fake letters, invisible characters, encoded instructions. Paste anything and see what's really there.

Runs entirely in your browser. Nothing you type is sent anywhere.

Loading the detector…

What this is. The same open-source detectors we use in front of AI models, compiled for the browser: deobfuscate 1.18.1 (undoes the hiding, step by step, and gives the verdict) and unicode-interference 1.0.0 (labels every character's alphabet and spots look-alikes). Both are MIT-licensed on crates.io.

What it isn't. It finds hidden text, not harmful meaning. A harmful instruction written in plain words passes clean, so in a real system this is one layer among several.
